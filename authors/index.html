How I'd set it up

  1. 1

    Audit

    Review the list, Klaviyo account and Shopify store. Clean the list, check deliverability, find what's already working.

  2. 2

    First offer

    Pick one ebook topic your readers already want, based on which books sold best and what they reply about. Price it as an easy first purchase.

  3. 3

    Buy and receive

    Shopify checkout with automatic ebook delivery the moment the order is paid. No manual sending.

  4. 4

    Flows

    Welcome, nurture, sales and post-purchase emails, written in a warm, personal voice. Value first, then the offer.

  5. 5

    Book launches

    A reusable Amazon launch sequence, plus a simple monthly campaign plan and reporting.
